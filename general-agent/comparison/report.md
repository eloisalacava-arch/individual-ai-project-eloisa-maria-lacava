## 6. Interpretation

- **imdb_case_study**: accuracy 0.970 (balanced 0.970) with kappa 0.940; majority class `positive` accounts for 51.5% of the analysed rows.
- **phrasebank_75agree**: accuracy 0.913 (balanced 0.913) with kappa 0.841; majority class `neutral` accounts for 60.7% of the analysed rows.
- The lowest balanced accuracy was on `phrasebank_75agree` (0.913). Balanced accuracy averages per-class recall, so a lower value means at least one category was recalled less well; it does not by itself identify which category, how many rows, or why.
- Qualitatively, the reason most concentrated in disagreements was `imdb_case_study` ambiguous reference label; `phrasebank_75agree` ambiguous reference label. These are hypotheses about one sample each, not established causes.
- **`imdb_case_study`** — The plan's hypothesis is tested against the star-derived IMDb proxy label. Verdict on the pre-specified hypothesis (`ambiguous_or_complex`): against the star-derived IMDb proxy label the hypothesis is lower accuracy in the hypothesised direction, but inconclusive (the interval includes zero) (risk difference +4.1 pp, 95% CI -2.0 to +14.4, Fisher p = 0.328); against the manual human annotation the hypothesis is significantly lower accuracy for the subgroup, supporting the hypothesis (risk difference +12.0 pp, 95% CI +4.7 to +23.8, Fisher p = 0.001).
- **`imdb_case_study`** — Three-way agreement between the AI prediction, manual human annotation and star-derived IMDb proxy label: of the 6 disagreements with manual human annotation, only 1 lies on rows where the two references agree; the rest sit between them (5 side with star-derived IMDb proxy label, 0 with neither).
- Higher accuracy on one study than another is not evidence that the task, the model, or the benchmark is easier. Task, class count, class prevalence, reference construction, and sample selection differ together.
- Kappa differences partly reflect prevalence differences: a dominant majority class changes chance agreement, so kappa is not a distribution-free effect size.
- No causal claim is made anywhere in this report. All statements are descriptive associations within one sample per study.

## 7. Limitations

- The reference is a benchmark label, not an adjudicated gold standard. Unless per-annotator labels are released, human-human kappa cannot be computed and model errors cannot be confirmed.
- Accuracy and recall use Wilson intervals; kappa, balanced accuracy, precision and F1 use a seeded percentile bootstrap.
- All intervals are conditional on this sample; the sample is not stratified.
- The studies are independent samples with different tasks, class counts, reference construction, class prevalence and sample size; their intervals overlap and no between-study test was performed, so between-study differences are descriptive.
- No causal claim is made. Statements are descriptive associations within one sample per study.
- Temperature is not controlled and model versions drift.
- `imdb_case_study`: The 200 predictions were produced by a previous run and are reused verbatim; this stage makes no model calls.
- `imdb_case_study`: The star-derived IMDb label comes from ratings, not from reading the text.
- `imdb_case_study`: Pretraining contamination with IMDb-like text cannot be ruled out.
- `imdb_case_study`: No human-human agreement estimate exists for the manual annotations.
- `phrasebank_75agree`: The sample is drawn from the >=75% annotator-agreement subset; sentences with weaker agreement are excluded by construction, so agreement with the majority label is plausibly overstated relative to the full corpus.
- `phrasebank_75agree`: Only 300 of the 3,453 available 75%-agreement sentences were sampled (unstratified, seed 20261116).
- `phrasebank_75agree`: Annotators came from one institution (Aalto University School of Business); agreement should be read with that in mind.
- `phrasebank_75agree`: The published agreement rate is item-level, not a pairwise kappa, so it is not directly comparable to Cohen's kappa.
- `phrasebank_75agree`: The benchmark is a majority verdict.
- `phrasebank_75agree`: Domain is English financial news from OMX Helsinki listed companies, collected before 2014.
- `phrasebank_75agree`: Licence is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0: non-commercial use only; data is not redistributed with this agent.
- Stability was checked only on a five-row pilot in one study.
